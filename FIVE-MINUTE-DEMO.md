# Five-minute live demonstration

1. **0:00–0:45 — Real baseline.** Open the default Malaysian 2026 case study. Show 20 zones, 11 valid and 9 insufficient at the illustrative 80% rule. Explain that LST/NDVI are historical composites and WorldCover is historical reference land cover. No live temperature or verified construction suitability is claimed.
2. **0:45–1:30 — Inspect the map.** Open Existing township. Choose Satellite only, then Surface temperature, NDVI and Heat hotspots. Show legends, opacity and the editable 44.78°C relative threshold. Insufficient polygons remain gray/dashed. Satellite imagery depends on permitted MapTiler access; street/offline fallback preserves the actual polygons.
3. **1:30–2:30 — Declare assumptions.** Click Try Example Planning Scenario. Point out DEMONSTRATION ASSUMPTIONS — NOT VERIFIED SITE DATA. Three hypothetical sites use actual observed environmental indicators but separate editable areas/resources. Follow a Zone ID to edit its site form, then Review example recommendations.
4. **2:30–3:30 — Make trade-offs.** Inspect recommendation formulas, site conditions and required verification. At untouched equal weights and RM150,000, the example selects trees plus carports costing RM140,040. The +10% sensitivity rerun replaces the carport with a cool roof, costing RM16,280. Explain that the objective is maximum preference points subject to constraints, not maximum expenditure or measured impact. Changing objectives changes choices.
5. **3:30–4:15 — Compare future plans fairly.** Open Future township, select Equal Budget Comparison Mode and use common area/budget. Land allocations and intervention options remain different. Show original estimates and optimised combinations. Switch back to restore independent budgets. Rooftop Solar Installation counts PV; roof conflicts remain infeasible until resolved.
6. **4:15–5:00 — Evidence and next steps.** Open Impact & evidence. Distinguish baseline, planned quantities, cost/solar estimates and unmeasured outcomes. Open a pilot checklist: a verified claim without an evidence note contributes zero supported items. Export the JSON summary. Return to the guided panel and Reset Example to restore the earlier planning state.

Do not claim actual cooling, emissions reduction, biodiversity gains, legal permissions or construction approval. The prototype identifies opportunities and supports transparent preliminary choices.

## Final rehearsal checks

Use proposed/hypothetical when describing example roofs and parking; historical bare-land cover does not prove they exist. Evidence entered for one proposal is not carried forward as supported when its inputs change. Before judging, verify the newest Netlify deploy, refresh to the 20/11/9 case, check satellite access, run/reset the example once and export. Keep a street/offline-map demonstration available if tile service is unavailable. The prototype remains usable for observed polygon analysis without background imagery.
